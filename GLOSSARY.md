# NeuroLearn

Vocabulary for studying YouTube channels and consulting their preserved evidence.

## Language

**Channel Study**:
A scoped investigation of publications from a YouTube channel, with evidence preserved for later questions and learning.
_Avoid_: Channel subscription, when referring to an investigation.

**Study Scope**:
The agreed set of publications included in a particular Channel Study.
_Avoid_: Entire channel, when only a selected subset is included.

**Ordinary Video**:
A YouTube video publication that is neither a Short nor a live stream or its recording.
_Avoid_: Long video, when publication format rather than duration is intended.

**Knowledge Base**:
The preserved source text, source-linked analysis, and thematic index of a Channel Study.
_Avoid_: Summary, when referring to the complete retained collection.

**Visual Candidate**:
A source interval whose transcript or speaking context indicates that inspecting the picture may add material information.
_Avoid_: Verified demonstration, before visual inspection.

**Study-owned Media**:
Media downloaded or generated for an identified Channel Study, with ownership established for that study. Pre-existing user files and shared media are outside this category.

**Retained Evidence**:
Source records, transcript versions, analyses, and selected visual evidence deliberately preserved as part of the Knowledge Base.
_Avoid_: Temporary cache, when referring to evidence intended for future consultation.

**Qualified Completion**:
A completed study with explicitly recorded source limitations and an explicit outcome for every selected publication. It does not imply perfect transcription or independent factual verification.
_Avoid_: Fully verified, when unresolved source limitations remain.

**Evidence Link**:
A connection from an analytical claim or conclusion to the specific studied source interval supporting it.
_Avoid_: Bibliography entry, when a particular claim's support is intended.

**Source Faithfulness**:
Accurate representation of what a studied source says, including its speaker, context, conditions, and uncertainty.
_Avoid_: Factual correctness, when only agreement with the source has been checked.
